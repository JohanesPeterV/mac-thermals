# Third-party notices

The chip-specific sensor key mappings in `SensorCatalog.swift` are adapted from
[Stats](https://github.com/exelban/stats/blob/3969ffd7e73e242007fc69288a1f5d3b786509b8/Modules/Sensors/values.swift).
Stats also served as a reference for the reverse-engineered AppleSMC protocol.
No Stats binary or runtime dependency is included.

Battery and SSD key meanings were cross-checked against
[VirtualSMC's sensor key documentation](https://github.com/acidanthera/VirtualSMC/blob/master/Docs/SMCSensorKeys.txt).
These community mappings are not an Apple hardware specification or proof of
compatibility on every listed chip.
